Resonance

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 8, 2026

Resonance is a spectrum-guessing party game — a clue-giver describes where something falls on a spectrum, and everyone else has to read their mind, not find the "right" answer. This policy covers what we collect while you play, matchmake, and shop for packs.

01 Information we collect

Account & profile. Sign in with Apple, an email address, or as an anonymous guest, plus your chosen display name.

Match & gameplay data. The matches you queue for or join, your round and guess records, and resulting score events — needed to run live scoring and reveals, visible to the other players in your match.

Purchases & entitlements. If you buy a spectrum pack, we record the purchase and the resulting entitlement so it unlocks on your account. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

Microphone. If a round type uses a voice clue, we request microphone access for that moment only.

Safety data. If you block another player, we store that so you're never matched with them again. If you send a bug report or contact request, we store its contents to respond to you.

Device & diagnostic data. App version, rough device type, and crash logs.

02 How we use it

  • Run matchmaking, live rounds, guesses, and scoreboards.
  • Unlock the packs and entitlements you've purchased.
  • Keep blocked players out of your future matches.
  • Respond to bug reports and support requests.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We don't use your gameplay history for advertising, and we don't sell your data.

03 Where it's stored

Resonance runs on Supabase (hosted Postgres, Auth, Realtime), with row-level security scoping each player to their own matches, purchases, and reports. Data is encrypted in transit.

Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Realtime) Apple / Google (Sign-In & purchases) Microphone — round-specific, optional

04 Your choices

  • Play as an anonymous guest instead of using Sign in with Apple or email.
  • Block any player, at any time, from within a match.
  • Deny microphone access — voice-clue rounds simply won't be offered to you.
  • Request an export or deletion of your data at any time — see contact below.

05 Children's privacy

Resonance is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

06 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date above and, where required, notify you in-app.

Questions about your data? We're happy to help.
