ShiftDrop

Drag. Shift. Drop.

Start dragging any file in Explorer, press Shift, and a wheel opens under your cursor. Drop the file on a format and the converted copy lands right next to the original. Images, PDFs and Word files. Crop, redact and annotate too. Everything stays on your PC.

How it's made

Every devIndi app is made with AI. Not AI for the sake of it, and not a chatbot bolted on. Just useful utilities, built faster and polished further, so the boring parts of your day take fewer clicks.

01

Built with AI

Designed, written and tested alongside AI tools. That's how one person can ship software that would usually need a team.

02

Checked by a human

Every app is used, broken and refined by hand until it feels right. Nothing ships just because the code compiles.

03

Made to be simple

One job per app, done well. No feature sprawl, no dark patterns, and plain-language privacy policies.
