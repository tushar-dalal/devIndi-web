Task Manager · In Development

Waypoint

Do the work. Don't manage the app.

Tasks don't usually get forgotten because a to-do list is missing — they get lost once they drift outside your immediate attention, buried in a flat list that can't tell the difference between "not started," "actively paused," and "no longer relevant." Waypoint gives every task an honest lifecycle instead of one undifferentiated pile, backed by a real server so it's durable and portable across devices.

A task's lifecycle

Every task moves through explicit states instead of living in one flat, ever-growing list:

Active Suspended Completed Deleted

Suspended tasks are paused, not abandoned — they can be revived. Deleted tasks step out of the way without vanishing outright.
