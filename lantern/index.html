Lantern

A focus and time-blindness companion — not a task manager wearing a costume.

People with executive-function friction don't usually fail to plan — they fail to start, remember, and avoid overwhelm once a list gets long. Most to-do apps make that worse: forced categorization at capture time, endless backlogs on one screen, and guilt-coded "3 overdue" language. Lantern's whole job is to remove that friction, then quietly look like a task manager underneath.

01 / The daily loop

01

Capture

Drop a thought in, typed or spoken — no fields, no categorizing, no friction.

02

Cap today

Pick only what actually fits today. Everything else waits, out of sight.

03

Nudge

A gentle, well-timed reminder — never a shame-coded "overdue" badge.

04

Carry forward

What didn't get done simply rolls to tomorrow, no penalty, no red ink.
