Chatpata

"Sach bolo, ya jhooth ghadho." — Tell the truth, or cook up a lie.

A real-time multiplayer bluffing trivia game built entirely on Indian trivia. Everyone sees the same obscure-but-real prompt, invents a fake-but-plausible answer, and votes on which one — among all the lies and the one truth — is actually real. Fool your friends, spot the truth, screenshot the chaos.

Party Game

Not released yet. Chatpata is experimental and being tested privately. It isn't available to download, and details may change before launch.

01 / How a round works

01

Prompt reveal

Everyone sees the same fill-in-the-blank trivia prompt at the same moment.

02

Bluff writing

Each player privately writes a plausible fake answer — nobody sees anyone else's submission.

03

Voting

All the bluffs and the one real answer are shuffled together anonymously. Everyone votes for the one they think is true.

04

Reveal & scoreboard

The truth is revealed, bluffers are unmasked, points animate in, and the leaderboard updates before the next round.

02 / What makes it Chatpata

01

India-only content

Every prompt is real Indian trivia — history, food, cinema, geography — nothing generic or imported.

02

Busted Cards

Get caught bluffing and your reaction photo can join the round recap, just for your room.

03

Custom questions

Write your own prompts to fold into future rounds for your group.

04

Real-time rooms

Create a room, share the code, and play live with everyone's screens moving in lockstep.

Data collected while you play, explained plainly.
