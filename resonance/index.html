Resonance

One clue. One dial. The fun is reading minds, not being "right."

Resonance is a spectrum-guessing party game played in person around a physical dial — a spectrum stretched between two opposing concepts, a hidden target somewhere along it, one player giving a single clue, and everyone else racing to place a pointer as close as they can before the reveal. It only ever worked with everyone crowded around one table. Resonance keeps the tension of that single silent clue and drops the table.

How a round works

1

The Psychic sees the target

One player alone sees where the hidden target sits on this round's spectrum.

2

One clue, no more

The Psychic gives a single word or phrase meant to point everyone toward the target — and nothing else.

3

Everyone places a pointer

Each player drags their own pointer onto their own phone's spectrum before the clock runs out.

4

The dial reveals

The target and everyone's guesses light up together, closest scores highest, and the Psychic rotates for the next round.
