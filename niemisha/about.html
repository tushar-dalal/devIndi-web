About

I'm Niemisha.

I'm an artist and a curator of art and objects. In the studio I work with gouache, clay and paper, mostly on small, quiet pieces about light, rooms and the things we keep.

Outside it, I bring other makers together: group shows, pop-ups and collections of objects that deserve a closer look.
