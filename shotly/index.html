Shotly

Press. Drag. Done.

Shotly takes over your Print Screen key and turns every screenshot into something you can find, fix and share in seconds. Capture anything, hide anything private, copy the text out of any picture, and search every screenshot you've ever taken by the words inside it.

04 / Hide anything private

Share the screenshot, not your secrets. One click reads the picture and pixelates everything sensitive: emails, phone numbers, card numbers, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, IP addresses, API keys, passwords and one-time codes.

It pixelates rather than blurs, so the hidden text can't be sharpened back. Change your mind? Ctrl + Z.

07 / Private by design

  • Everything happens on your PC. No account, no cloud, no uploads. Your screenshots never leave your computer.
  • Text is read by the OCR built into Windows, so even search works fully offline.
  • Shotly never moves or changes your existing screenshots. It only reads them to make them searchable.
  • Deleting a capture sends it to the Recycle Bin, so a slip of the finger is never permanent.
  • Like every devIndi app, Shotly is made with AI, then used daily and refined by hand.
